# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step)

## Users

People seeking guidance, reflection, or clarity on a question in their life (relationships, career, decisions, self-understanding), who want a tarot reading on their own schedule rather than booking or waiting for a human reader.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered tarot reading app: the user asks a question or picks a spread, the AI draws cards and generates a personalized interpretation in the moment. This landing page's job is to get a first-time visitor to trust the concept and start a reading.

## Positioning

Always-on, personalized AI tarot reader — distinct from generic horoscope/daily-card apps (impersonal, one-size-fits-all) and from human-reader booking apps (scheduling friction, waiting, per-session cost). The mechanism a competitor can't casually copy: instant, contextual interpretation generated for the visitor's actual question, available any time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core loop: ask a question → draw card(s) → receive an AI-generated interpretation.
- No human readers involved in the core product.
- Undecided/open: pricing model (free/freemium/subscription), specific spreads offered, account requirement before first reading, native app vs. web-only.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing copy, testimonials, brand assets, or product screenshots. This is a from-scratch landing page; do not fabricate testimonials, user counts, press mentions, or pricing claims. Placeholder/generic supporting copy is acceptable where explicitly marked, but no invented "proof."

## Product Principles

- Instant and personal beats scheduled and generic — every CTA and headline should sell immediacy and personalization, not "browse readers."
- Respect the visitor's skepticism: modern, credible design over mystical kitsch, since the AI-driven mechanism is the actual differentiator, not costume.
- One primary action: start a reading. Secondary content (how it works, trust, spreads) supports that single conversion path rather than competing with it.
- Never claim certainty or fortune-telling as fact; the tone is reflective guidance, not prediction-as-truth.
